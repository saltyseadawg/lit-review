# Summary

Created a CAPT tool for learning Mandarin tones and used it on Mandarin learners.

------------------------------------

# Notes
### Research questions


1. Is automated feedback provided by the CAPT system effective in guiding self-directed Mandarin tonal pronunciation learning compared to traditional listen-and-repeat methods? 

2. How do learners develop tonal awareness and proficiency using a CAPT system in sentence-level contexts, and what are their perceptions of the system’s effectiveness?


### Findings
- large increase in post test scores in treatment group
- >70% of CAPT group agreed that Mandarin tone understanding and pronunication improved
- >70% of CAPT group found automatic evaluation and feedback helpful

### Methodology
- 17 students in elementary level Mandarin course in China
	- roughly A1+ CEFR level
- 2 groups
	- control
		- listen and repeat task, no feedback
	- treatment
		- used CAPT tool
- 10 minutes of daily practice everyday with CAPT tool for 2 weeks
	- 10 days (2 business weeks i guess)
- 10 minute tutorial video provided
- pre test and post test done
- survey at end
